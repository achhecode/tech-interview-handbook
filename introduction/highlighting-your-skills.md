## How do you highlight your strongest skills without sounding boastful?

Medium
{: .label .label-yellow }

**Answer**

Use **Claim, Evidence, Impact**:

- **Claim:** "I'm strong in designing REST APIs."
- **Evidence:** "I designed the order APIs for a banking product with 4 integrations."
- **Impact:** "It reduced integration issues and onboarding time for partners."

**Key points**
- Pick three skills that match the job description.
- Use numbers: latency, cost, uptime, team size, release frequency.
- Say "I" for your work and "we" for team outcomes.

**Follow-up questions**
- What is your weakest technical area and how are you improving it?
- Which skill on your resume would you rate 7/10 and why?
