## Tell me about yourself.

Easy
{: .label .label-green }

**Answer**

Use the **Present, Past, Future** formula and keep it to 60 to 90 seconds.

1. **Present:** current role, years of experience, core stack.
2. **Past:** two or three relevant career highlights with measurable results.
3. **Future:** why this role and company fit what you want next.

**Sample script** (replace the placeholders)

> I'm a Senior Software Engineer with [X] years of experience building Java and Spring Boot microservices. Currently at [Company], I own [module/system], which handles [scale or domain]. Earlier I worked on [domain 1] and [domain 2], where I [achievement with number, e.g. cut API latency by 40%]. I enjoy designing backend systems and mentoring juniors. I'm now looking for a role where I can [goal], and [Company] stood out because [reason].

**Key points**
- Do not recite your resume line by line.
- End with a hook the interviewer can pick up, such as "I led a Kafka migration".

{: .trap }
Avoid personal history, long career timelines, and negative remarks about past employers.
