## How do you explain your academic background, including gaps or average grades?

Easy
{: .label .label-green }

**Answer**

Keep it brief, factual and forward-looking.

1. State degree, university and year in one sentence.
2. If there is a gap or low score, give a one-line reason, then pivot to what you did next.
3. Link your learning to your work: certifications, side projects, open-source repositories.

**Example**
> I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science in 2019. Since then my learning has been mostly on the job and through projects, such as [project], and I hold [certification].

{: .tip }
Interviewers care far more about recent work than about your degree after your first job.
