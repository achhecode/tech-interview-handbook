## Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.

Medium
{: .label .label-yellow }

**Answer**

Use **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

- **Situation:** two options for a caching approach, and we disagreed.
- **Task:** decide quickly without hurting the team.
- **Action:** compared both with a small benchmark and shared data in the review.
- **Result:** we picked the option backed by data and release stayed on schedule.

{: .tip }
Show that you disagree with ideas, not people, and that you commit once the team decides.

## Why are you looking for a change?

Easy
{: .label .label-green }

**Answer**

Focus on growth, not complaints: "I've built strong expertise in X and want to work on Y at a larger scale." Never criticise your current employer.

---

## Do you have any questions for us?

Easy
{: .label .label-green }

**Answer**

Always ask. Good questions:
- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- How does the team handle production incidents and on-call?
- What is the current tech debt, and how is it prioritised?
