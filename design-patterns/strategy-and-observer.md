## What are the Strategy and Observer patterns?

Medium
{: .label .label-yellow }

**Answer**

**Strategy** encapsulates interchangeable algorithms behind an interface and selects one at runtime (payment methods, discount rules). In Java 8+, a lambda can be a strategy. **Observer** lets subscribers be notified when a subject changes (Spring `ApplicationEvent` and `@EventListener`).
